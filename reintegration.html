Reintegration Program

The Reintegration Program of the Cagayan Provincial Jail supports the preparation of Persons Deprived of Liberty (PDLs) for their eventual return to their families and communities. The program recognizes that rehabilitation extends beyond custodial care and includes preparation for responsible, productive, and peaceful participation in society.

Through coordinated rehabilitation, education, skills development, values formation, health and wellness, livelihood, family support, and other appropriate interventions, the program seeks to help PDLs develop the knowledge, skills, attitudes, and support systems that may assist them in adjusting to life after release.

Objectives of the Program

The Reintegration Program seeks to:

  1. Prepare PDLs for Return to the Community
    Help qualified PDLs prepare for the responsibilities, opportunities, and challenges associated with returning to their families and communities.
  2. Strengthen Family and Social Connections
    Encourage the maintenance and strengthening of positive relationships with family members and other appropriate support networks.
  3. Promote Education and Skills Development
    Encourage PDLs to continue education, technical-vocational training, livelihood development, and other appropriate learning opportunities that may support their future.
  4. Develop Responsible Life Skills
    Strengthen communication, decision-making, problem-solving, personal responsibility, planning, and other practical life skills.
  5. Encourage Productive and Law-Abiding Participation
    Promote positive values, responsible behavior, peaceful relationships, and constructive participation in family and community life.

Reintegration Support Activities

Reintegration may involve various appropriate activities and interventions coordinated with PDLs, families, government agencies, local government units, community organizations, and other stakeholders. These may include:

  1. Individual Reintegration Planning
    Encourages qualified PDLs to identify personal goals, education and skills needs, family responsibilities, livelihood interests, and other areas that may support their transition after release.
  2. Family Support and Communication
    Promotes appropriate family communication and support to help maintain positive relationships and strengthen family connections.
  3. Education and Skills Continuity
    Encourages PDLs to identify possible opportunities for continuing education, technical-vocational training, skills development, or other learning activities after release.
  4. Livelihood and Employment Preparation
    Supports awareness of appropriate livelihood, employment, skills utilization, and other productive opportunities that may be available in the community.
  5. Values and Life Skills Development
    Reinforces positive values, responsible decision-making, communication, conflict management, discipline, and other life skills that may support successful community participation.
  6. Health and Wellness Continuity
    Encourages appropriate attention to continuing health needs, health information, referrals, and wellness practices when necessary and applicable.
  7. Referral and Community Linkages
    Promotes coordination and referral with appropriate government agencies, local government units, service providers, organizations, and other community partners that may provide support after release.

Reintegration as the Continuing Goal of Rehabilitation

Reintegration is closely connected with the other rehabilitation programs of the Cagayan Provincial Jail. Education provides opportunities for learning, skills development builds practical competencies, health and wellness supports overall well-being, values formation promotes positive attitudes and responsible behavior, and livelihood activities encourage productivity.

By bringing these efforts together, the Reintegration Program seeks to help qualified PDLs prepare for a constructive transition from custody to community life. The program also recognizes the importance of family, community support, continuing education, livelihood, employment, and access to appropriate services.

The Cagayan Provincial Jail continues to support rehabilitation initiatives that provide PDLs with meaningful opportunities for personal development and preparation for responsible participation in their families and communities upon release.

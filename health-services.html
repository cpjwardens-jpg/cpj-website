Health Services

The Health Services of the Cagayan Provincial Jail support the delivery of appropriate health care and health-related services to Persons Deprived of Liberty (PDLs) while in custody. The service promotes access to medical and dental care, preventive health measures, health education, monitoring, referral, and other appropriate interventions that contribute to the health, well-being, and humane care of PDLs.

Health services are implemented through the coordination of jail health personnel, qualified medical and dental professionals, government health agencies, hospitals, local government units, partner organizations, and other appropriate stakeholders, subject to available resources and applicable policies and procedures.

Objectives of Health Services

  1. Promote Access to Appropriate Health Care
    Support access to appropriate medical, dental, preventive, and wellness services for PDLs.
  2. Promote Disease Prevention
    Encourage proper hygiene, sanitation, health education, preventive practices, and appropriate health monitoring.
  3. Support Early Identification of Health Concerns
    Promote appropriate health screening, consultation, monitoring, and follow-up to help identify health concerns requiring attention.
  4. Facilitate Referral and Continuity of Care
    Coordinate appropriate referral to hospitals, clinics, specialists, and other health facilities when additional examination or treatment is required.
  5. Promote Health Awareness and Wellness
    Encourage PDLs to develop knowledge and practices that support personal health, hygiene, nutrition, and overall wellness.

Core Health Services

  1. Medical Consultation and Primary Care
    Provides appropriate consultation, basic assessment, monitoring, treatment, and follow-up care based on professional evaluation and available services.
  2. Dental Services
    Supports appropriate dental examination, oral hygiene education, preventive care, treatment, and referral for dental concerns requiring additional attention.
  3. Health Screening and Monitoring
    Supports appropriate screening and monitoring to identify health concerns and facilitate timely intervention and follow-up.
  4. Preventive Health and Disease Control
    Promotes hygiene, sanitation, disease prevention, health awareness, and other appropriate measures to reduce health risks within the custodial environment.
  5. Health Education
    Provides appropriate information and awareness activities on hygiene, nutrition, disease prevention, healthy practices, and other relevant health concerns.
  6. Health Records and Documentation
    Supports appropriate documentation and maintenance of health information to assist in monitoring, continuity of care, and referral.
  7. Emergency Health Response
    Supports appropriate initial response, coordination, and referral during medical emergencies and other urgent health situations.
  8. Referral and Hospital Coordination
    Coordinates appropriate referral and follow-up with hospitals, clinics, medical professionals, dental professionals, and other health facilities when needed.

Health, Hygiene and Wellness Promotion

Health Services also promote health awareness among PDLs through appropriate information, education, and wellness activities. These efforts may include personal hygiene, sanitation, nutrition, disease prevention, healthy lifestyle practices, and responsible health-seeking behavior.

Health Services as Part of Rehabilitation

Good health supports the effective participation of PDLs in education, skills development, values formation, livelihood, and other rehabilitation activities. For this reason, health care is an important part of the overall rehabilitation environment of the Cagayan Provincial Jail.

By promoting appropriate health care, preventive practices, health education, wellness, and continuity of care, the service supports the physical and overall well-being of PDLs while in custody.

Through coordination with health professionals, government agencies, hospitals, local government units, and other appropriate partners, the Cagayan Provincial Jail seeks to strengthen health support for PDLs and contribute to humane custody, rehabilitation, personal development, and successful reintegration.

ABOUT US

Cagayan Provincial Jail

Cagayan Provincial Jail is committed to providing secure, safe, and humane custody while promoting the dignity, welfare, rehabilitation, and personal development of Persons Deprived of Liberty (PDLs). Through education, skills development, health and wellness, values formation, and meaningful rehabilitation programs, CPJ provides opportunities that support positive change, personal growth, and successful reintegration into families and communities.

Our Commitment

To provide humane custody, rehabilitation, education, skills development, and meaningful opportunities for PDLs.

Our Mission

To support the safe custody, personal development, and successful reintegration of PDLs into their families and communities.

OUR PROGRAMS

Rehabilitation and Development Programs

Cagayan Provincial Jail provides programs that support education, skills development, health, values formation, livelihood, and successful reintegration of Persons Deprived of Liberty.

Education

Provides learning opportunities that support literacy, academic development, and continuing education.

Skills Development

Provides technical-vocational training and practical skills to prepare PDLs for productive opportunities.

Health and Wellness

Promotes physical, mental, and overall wellness through appropriate health and preventive services.

Values Formation

Supports positive values, responsible behavior, personal growth, and character development.

Livelihood

Develops practical livelihood skills and meaningful activities that may support future self-sufficiency.

Reintegration

Helps prepare PDLs for successful return to their families and communities.

OUR SERVICES

Services of Cagayan Provincial Jail

Cagayan Provincial Jail provides essential custodial, health, legal, and support services to promote the safety, welfare, and development of Persons Deprived of Liberty.

Secure Custody

Maintains safe, secure, humane, and orderly custody of Persons Deprived of Liberty.

Health Services

Provides appropriate medical, dental, preventive, and wellness services to support the health of PDLs.

Welfare and Development Services

Supports rehabilitation, education, skills development, values formation, livelihood, and other developmental activities for PDLs.

Visitor and Support Services

Facilitates appropriate visitation, communication, coordination, and support for PDLs and their families.

NEWS & ANNOUNCEMENTS

Latest Updates from Cagayan Provincial Jail

Education Program

CPJ continues to provide meaningful education and learning opportunities for Persons Deprived of Liberty, supporting learners from basic literacy and elementary education through Junior High School and Senior High School under the Alternative Learning System (ALS). Through these educational opportunities, CPJ promotes lifelong learning, personal development, and continued access to education as part of its rehabilitation program.

Skills Development and Livelihood Program

Technical-vocational training helps Persons Deprived of Liberty develop practical and marketable skills for future livelihood and employment opportunities. CPJ, through strong partnerships with the TESDA Regional and Provincial Training Centers and Aparri Polytechnic Institute, provides free skills training, assessment, and certification to support the personal development and successful reintegration of PDLs.

Rehabilitation Program

CPJ continues to implement a comprehensive rehabilitation program that supports the personal development and well-being of Persons Deprived of Liberty. The program includes health and wellness, recreational and sports activities, cultural activities, religious activities, and other meaningful interventions that promote positive values, personal growth, and the constructive use of time.

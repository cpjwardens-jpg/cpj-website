Welfare and Development Services

The Welfare and Development Services of the Cagayan Provincial Jail support the overall rehabilitation, personal development, and well-being of Persons Deprived of Liberty (PDLs) while in custody. The service provides opportunities for PDLs to participate in educational, developmental, recreational, social, cultural, spiritual, and other appropriate activities that promote constructive personal growth.

These services complement custodial management and other rehabilitation programs by addressing appropriate social, developmental, and personal needs of PDLs. Through coordination with jail personnel, government agencies, local government units, volunteers, religious organizations, civil society groups, and other stakeholders, the service seeks to create meaningful opportunities for positive development while in custody.

Objectives of the Service

  1. Support PDL Welfare and Well-Being
    Promote appropriate activities and services that contribute to the physical, emotional, social, and overall well-being of PDLs.
  2. Promote Rehabilitation and Personal Development
    Provide meaningful opportunities for learning, self-development, positive behavior, and constructive use of time.
  3. Strengthen Family and Social Relationships
    Encourage appropriate family connections, positive interpersonal relationships, communication, and social support.
  4. Encourage Positive Participation
    Provide opportunities for PDLs to participate in appropriate programs, activities, and initiatives that promote responsibility, cooperation, discipline, and personal growth.
  5. Strengthen Community Partnerships
    Encourage coordination with government agencies, organizations, volunteers, and other stakeholders that can support appropriate welfare and development activities.

Welfare and Development Activities

The service may support and coordinate various appropriate activities based on the needs of PDLs, available resources, and applicable policies and procedures. These may include:

  1. Education and Learning Support
    Supports access to appropriate educational programs, reading activities, learning opportunities, and other academic or enrichment activities.
  2. Skills and Livelihood Development
    Supports participation in technical-vocational, practical skills, livelihood, and other productive activities that contribute to rehabilitation.
  3. Values Formation and Personal Development
    Encourages activities that promote positive values, responsible behavior, character development, reflection, and personal growth.
  4. Recreational and Wellness Activities
    Supports appropriate recreational, physical, cultural, and wellness activities that promote healthy interaction, positive use of time, and overall well-being.
  5. Spiritual and Faith-Based Activities
    Facilitates appropriate religious and spiritual activities through authorized religious organizations, ministers, volunteers, and partners while observing applicable jail policies and respecting individual beliefs.
  6. Family Support and Communication
    Supports appropriate opportunities for maintaining family relationships and communication consistent with applicable visitation and communication policies.
  7. Social and Cultural Activities
    Encourages appropriate cultural, social, creative, and community-oriented activities that promote cooperation, respect, creativity, and positive interaction.
  8. Volunteer and Community Participation
    Coordinates appropriate participation of volunteers, organizations, civic groups, and other partners in approved welfare and development activities.

Support for PDL Development

Welfare and development activities provide PDLs with opportunities to learn, participate, interact positively with others, discover interests, develop abilities, and use their time constructively. These activities may also help strengthen confidence, responsibility, cooperation, discipline, and positive social relationships.

Welfare and Development as Part of Rehabilitation

Rehabilitation involves more than custodial supervision. It also requires meaningful opportunities for personal development, learning, positive interaction, and constructive participation. Welfare and development services help provide an environment where PDLs can participate in appropriate activities that support their rehabilitation journey.

These services work together with education, skills development, health and wellness, values formation, livelihood, and reintegration programs. Through this coordinated approach, the Cagayan Provincial Jail seeks to address appropriate developmental needs while maintaining safe, secure, and humane custody.

Through continuing welfare and development initiatives, the Cagayan Provincial Jail seeks to promote personal growth, responsible behavior, meaningful participation, and preparation for productive and responsible reintegration into families and communities.

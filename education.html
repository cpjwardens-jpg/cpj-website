Jail-Based Education Program

The Jail-Based Education Program of the Cagayan Provincial Jail provides qualified Persons Deprived of Liberty (PDLs) with accessible and meaningful opportunities to continue their education while in custody. The program supports basic literacy, elementary education, junior high school, senior high school, and other appropriate learning pathways through formal and alternative education programs.

Specific Learning Pathways

The program provides educational opportunities through the following learning pathways:

  1. Basic Literacy Education
    Provides foundational learning in reading, writing, numeracy, comprehension, and other essential skills for learners who need to strengthen their basic literacy competencies.
  2. Elementary Education
    Provides qualified learners with opportunities to develop and complete elementary-level competencies through appropriate formal or alternative learning programs.
  3. Junior High School Education
    Supports qualified learners in continuing their junior high school education and developing academic knowledge, life skills, and competencies necessary for further learning.
  4. Senior High School Education
    Provides qualified learners with opportunities to pursue senior high school education and acquire competencies that may support further education, skills development, employment, and community reintegration.
  5. Alternative Learning System (ALS)
    Provides flexible learning opportunities for qualified PDLs who are unable to access or complete the regular formal education system. ALS may provide appropriate pathways toward the completion of basic education, subject to applicable program requirements.
  6. Continuing and Enrichment Learning
    Encourages learners to participate in appropriate educational, reading, self-learning, and enrichment activities that promote lifelong learning, personal development, and positive use of time while in custody.

Education as Part of Rehabilitation

Through these learning pathways, the Jail-Based Education Program seeks to help PDLs pursue their educational goals, strengthen their knowledge and skills, develop confidence and positive attitudes, and prepare for productive and responsible participation in their families and communities upon reintegration.

By providing opportunities to learn and grow while in custody, the program contributes to the broader goals of rehabilitation, personal development, and successful reintegration of PDLs.

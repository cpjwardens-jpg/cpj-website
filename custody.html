Secure Custody Service

The Secure Custody Service of the Cagayan Provincial Jail is responsible for maintaining the safe, secure, orderly, and humane custody of Persons Deprived of Liberty (PDLs). It supports the implementation of appropriate custodial procedures while respecting the dignity, rights, and welfare of persons under custody.

The service is carried out by trained jail personnel through established security procedures, supervision, monitoring, documentation, and coordination with appropriate authorities. These efforts help maintain a safe custodial environment for PDLs, jail personnel, visitors, and other authorized persons.

Core Custodial Services

  1. Custodial Supervision
    Provides continuous supervision and appropriate monitoring of PDLs within the jail facility.
  2. Security and Safety Measures
    Implements appropriate security procedures intended to maintain order and prevent security incidents within the facility.
  3. Movement and Escort Services
    Facilitates authorized movement and escort of PDLs in accordance with established custodial procedures.
  4. Facility Security
    Supports the security and proper management of jail facilities, including appropriate access control and monitoring.
  5. Emergency Preparedness
    Supports preparedness and coordinated response to emergencies, security incidents, and other situations requiring immediate action.
  6. Documentation and Reporting
    Maintains appropriate records and reports related to custodial operations, security incidents, movements, and other relevant activities.
  7. Coordination with Authorities
    Coordinates with appropriate government agencies, law enforcement authorities, courts, and other authorized stakeholders when required.

Humane and Professional Custody

Secure custody is implemented together with the principle of humane treatment. Jail personnel are expected to perform their duties professionally while maintaining respect for the dignity and welfare of PDLs and ensuring the orderly implementation of custodial policies and procedures.

A safe and orderly custodial environment also provides a stable setting where PDLs can participate in education, skills development, health and wellness, values formation, livelihood, and other rehabilitation programs.

Values Formation Program

The Values Formation Program of the Cagayan Provincial Jail is a rehabilitation initiative that promotes positive values, responsible behavior, character development, and personal growth among Persons Deprived of Liberty (PDLs). The program provides meaningful opportunities for PDLs to reflect on their experiences, attitudes, decisions, responsibilities, relationships, and goals for positive change while in custody.

Through values education, character development, spiritual and faith-based activities, counseling and reflection, life-skills activities, and other appropriate interventions, the program encourages PDLs to develop respect, responsibility, discipline, honesty, compassion, accountability, and other positive values that may support constructive behavior and responsible participation in their families and communities.

Objectives of the Program

The Values Formation Program seeks to:

  1. Promote Positive Values
    Encourage the development of honesty, respect, responsibility, discipline, compassion, integrity, and other positive values.
  2. Strengthen Character and Personal Responsibility
    Help PDLs recognize their responsibilities, reflect on their choices, and develop a stronger sense of accountability for their actions and decisions.
  3. Encourage Positive Behavioral Change
    Support the development of constructive attitudes, self- discipline, peaceful interaction, cooperation, and responsible behavior within the custodial environment.
  4. Develop Life Skills
    Strengthen communication, decision-making, problem-solving, emotional awareness, interpersonal relationships, and other practical life skills.
  5. Strengthen Family and Community Relationships
    Encourage PDLs to recognize the importance of family, respectful relationships, social responsibility, and positive participation in their communities.

Values Formation Activities

The program may be implemented through various structured, educational, spiritual, personal development, and community- oriented activities, including:

  1. Values Education and Character Formation
    Provides learning and reflection activities that promote positive values, ethical decision-making, responsibility, respect, and good character.
  2. Spiritual and Faith-Based Activities
    Supports appropriate religious and spiritual activities facilitated by authorized religious organizations, ministers, volunteers, and partners, while respecting applicable jail policies and individual beliefs.
  3. Personal Reflection and Self-Development
    Provides opportunities for PDLs to reflect on their life experiences, decisions, responsibilities, strengths, areas for improvement, and goals for positive personal change.
  4. Life Skills Development
    Develops practical skills in communication, decision-making, problem-solving, conflict management, responsible behavior, and positive interpersonal relationships.
  5. Family and Relationship Development
    Promotes respect, communication, responsibility, and positive family relationships that may help strengthen connections between PDLs and their families.
  6. Responsible Citizenship and Community Awareness
    Encourages awareness of social responsibilities, respect for others, peaceful participation, cooperation, and constructive involvement in the community.
  7. Peer Support and Positive Interaction
    Encourages cooperation, mutual respect, peaceful relationships, teamwork, and positive peer interaction within the jail community.
  8. Reintegration and Future Planning
    Encourages PDLs to reflect on their goals, responsibilities, family relationships, livelihood, education, skills, and other areas that may support positive and responsible reintegration into the community.

Values Formation as Part of Rehabilitation

Values formation is an important part of the rehabilitation process because positive change involves not only acquiring knowledge and skills, but also developing responsible attitudes, values, and behavior. The program provides PDLs with opportunities to reflect, learn, improve relationships, and work toward constructive personal development.

The program complements education, skills development, health and wellness, livelihood, and other rehabilitation initiatives by helping PDLs develop positive attitudes and values that may support responsible decision-making and productive participation in their families and communities.

Through continuing values formation and personal development activities, the Cagayan Provincial Jail seeks to help PDLs prepare for responsible, peaceful, productive, and meaningful participation in society upon their reintegration.

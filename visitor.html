Visitor and Support Services

The Visitor and Support Services of the Cagayan Provincial Jail facilitate appropriate visitation, communication, coordination, and other authorized forms of support for Persons Deprived of Liberty (PDLs) and their families. These services recognize the importance of maintaining positive family and social connections while ensuring that visitation and communication are conducted in a safe, secure, orderly, and humane manner.

The service is implemented in accordance with applicable jail policies, security procedures, visitation rules, and other requirements. It involves coordination among jail personnel, PDLs, family members, authorized visitors, government agencies, and other appropriate stakeholders.

Objectives of the Service

  1. Facilitate Appropriate Visitation
    Support the orderly and secure conduct of authorized visits between PDLs and their families or other permitted visitors.
  2. Maintain Family Connections
    Encourage appropriate communication and continued family relationships that may provide emotional and social support to PDLs.
  3. Maintain Safe and Orderly Visitation
    Ensure that visitation activities follow established identification, access, security, scheduling, and facility procedures.
  4. Provide Appropriate Information and Assistance
    Assist visitors and families by providing appropriate information regarding applicable visitation procedures, requirements, schedules, and facility rules.
  5. Strengthen Support Networks
    Encourage appropriate family, community, organizational, and institutional support that may contribute to the welfare and rehabilitation of PDLs.

Visitor Services

  1. Authorized Visitation
    Facilitates visits by qualified and authorized family members, relatives, and other permitted visitors in accordance with applicable rules and procedures.
  2. Visitor Identification and Access Procedures
    Supports appropriate visitor identification, registration, screening, access control, and other established security procedures.
  3. Visitation Scheduling and Coordination
    Supports the orderly scheduling and coordination of authorized visitation activities based on applicable facility procedures.
  4. Visitor Information and Assistance
    Provides appropriate information and assistance concerning visitation procedures, requirements, schedules, and applicable facility policies.
  5. Family Communication Support
    Supports appropriate communication between PDLs and their families through authorized channels and procedures.
  6. Coordination with Authorized Organizations
    Facilitates appropriate coordination with authorized organizations, volunteers, religious groups, government agencies, and other stakeholders providing approved support services.
  7. Visitor Safety and Order
    Supports a safe, orderly, respectful, and secure environment during authorized visitation and other approved activities.

Family and Community Support

Family and positive social relationships can provide important emotional and social support during a person's period of custody. Appropriate communication and visitation may help maintain meaningful relationships and encourage continued support for rehabilitation and personal development.

The Cagayan Provincial Jail also recognizes the importance of coordination with appropriate government agencies, community organizations, volunteers, religious groups, and other stakeholders that may provide approved assistance and developmental support to PDLs.

Visitor and Support Services as Part of Rehabilitation

Rehabilitation is strengthened when PDLs are able to maintain appropriate and positive connections with their families and communities. Supportive relationships may encourage responsibility, motivation, personal growth, and preparation for eventual reintegration.

Visitor and Support Services therefore work alongside education, skills development, health and wellness, values formation, livelihood, and reintegration programs. Appropriate family and community support can complement these rehabilitation efforts while maintaining necessary security and custodial requirements.

Through safe, orderly, and humane visitation and support services, the Cagayan Provincial Jail seeks to promote positive relationships, strengthen appropriate support networks, and contribute to the rehabilitation and successful reintegration of PDLs.

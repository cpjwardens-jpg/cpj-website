Skills Development Program

Skills Development Training Program

The Skills Development Training Program of the Cagayan Provincial Jail provides qualified Persons Deprived of Liberty (PDLs) with opportunities to acquire technical-vocational, practical, and livelihood skills while in custody. The program is an important component of the jail's rehabilitation efforts, providing meaningful learning experiences that help PDLs develop knowledge, competencies, work values, and practical skills that may support employment, livelihood, and successful reintegration into their families and communities.

Technical-Vocational Education and Training

The program facilitates Technical-Vocational Education and Training (TVET) opportunities through coordination and partnership with the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA), including its Regional and Provincial Training Centers, as well as other training institutions such as the Aparri Polytechnic Institute.

Through these partnerships, qualified PDLs are given opportunities to participate in competency-based training, hands-on practical activities, competency assessment, and certification in accordance with applicable training standards and program requirements.

TESDA Training, Assessment and Competency Certification

TESDA supports the skills development of qualified PDLs by providing access to technical-vocational training, competency assessment, and competency certification opportunities. Training opportunities may be provided without training fees, subject to applicable program requirements, available training slots, assessment schedules, and other applicable conditions.

Through these opportunities, PDLs may acquire industry-relevant competencies and, upon meeting the required competency standards, obtain appropriate TESDA certification. Such competencies and certifications may help strengthen their opportunities for employment, livelihood, further skills development, and productive participation in the community after release.

Skills Training Programs

The Cagayan Provincial Jail, in coordination with its training partners, provides or facilitates various technical-vocational and livelihood-oriented training programs. These include:

  1. Electrical Installation and Maintenance NC II
    Develops competencies in electrical installation, maintenance, basic troubleshooting, workplace safety, and the proper use of electrical tools and equipment.
  2. Carpentry NC II
    Develops practical competencies in carpentry, measurement, preparation of materials, use of hand and power tools, and appropriate construction activities.
  3. Tile Setting NC II
    Provides hands-on training in surface preparation, tile installation, finishing, and other appropriate tile-setting activities.
  4. Bread and Pastry Production NC II
    Develops practical skills in the preparation, production, presentation, and proper handling of bread, pastries, and related bakery products.
  5. Housekeeping NC II
    Develops competencies in cleaning and maintaining rooms and facilities, housekeeping procedures, sanitation, safety, and related service activities.
  6. Cookery NC II
    Provides practical training in food preparation, cooking techniques, kitchen operations, sanitation, food safety, and proper handling of kitchen tools and equipment.
  7. Photovoltaic Systems Installation NC II
    Develops practical competencies related to the installation, operation, maintenance, and safety practices associated with photovoltaic solar energy systems, in accordance with applicable training standards.
  8. Hilot (Wellness Massage) NC II
    Provides training in appropriate wellness massage practices, client care, preparation, safety, and related competencies in accordance with applicable training standards.
  9. Masonry NC II
    Develops practical competencies in masonry work, preparation and handling of construction materials, measurement, construction techniques, and related building activities.
  10. Organic Vegetable Production
    Develops practical skills in the preparation, planting, cultivation, maintenance, harvesting, and basic management of organically grown vegetables, while promoting productive and livelihood-oriented agricultural activities.

Skills Training as Part of Rehabilitation

Skills training provides PDLs with meaningful opportunities to learn, practice, and strengthen practical competencies while in custody. It promotes productive use of time, discipline, responsibility, teamwork, creativity, and positive work values.

Through technical-vocational training, hands-on learning, competency assessment, and certification, the program seeks to help qualified PDLs prepare for possible employment, livelihood activities, further education and training, and other productive opportunities after release.

The Skills Development Training Program contributes to the broader rehabilitation and reintegration efforts of the Cagayan Provincial Jail by helping PDLs develop practical competencies and positive work values that may contribute to their personal development and future participation in their families and communities.

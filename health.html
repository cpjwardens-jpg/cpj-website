Health and Wellness Program

The Health and Wellness Program of the Cagayan Provincial Jail promotes the health, well-being, and humane care of Persons Deprived of Liberty (PDLs) while in custody. The program supports access to appropriate medical, dental, preventive, psychosocial, and wellness services, while promoting health education, personal hygiene, proper nutrition, disease prevention, and responsible health practices.

The program is implemented through the coordination of the Cagayan Provincial Jail Health Services, qualified health professionals, government health agencies, hospitals, local government units, partner organizations, and other stakeholders. Through these partnerships, the program seeks to strengthen access to appropriate health services and timely referral when additional medical or specialized care is needed.

Health and Wellness Services

The Health and Wellness Program supports a range of appropriate health services and activities designed to promote the physical, mental, emotional, and social well-being of PDLs.

  1. Medical Consultation and Primary Health Care
    Provides appropriate medical consultation, basic health assessment, monitoring, treatment, and follow-up care for PDLs with health concerns, subject to available services and professional assessment.
  2. Dental Health Services
    Promotes oral health through appropriate dental examination, preventive care, treatment, oral hygiene education, and referral for dental concerns requiring further attention.
  3. Preventive Health and Disease Control
    Promotes disease prevention through health education, personal hygiene, sanitation, appropriate health monitoring, and other preventive measures intended to reduce health risks within the custodial environment.
  4. Mental and Psychosocial Wellness
    Supports appropriate psychosocial interventions, counseling referrals, wellness activities, health education, and other interventions that promote emotional well-being, positive coping, healthy relationships, and personal development.
  5. Nutrition and Wellness
    Promotes proper nutrition, healthy lifestyle practices, physical wellness, and appropriate nutrition-related education to support the overall health and well-being of PDLs.
  6. Health Education and Health Promotion
    Provides health information and awareness activities on personal hygiene, nutrition, disease prevention, sanitation, healthy practices, and other relevant health concerns.
  7. Health Screening and Monitoring
    Supports appropriate health screening, monitoring, and identification of health concerns to facilitate timely intervention, follow-up, and referral when necessary.
  8. Health Records and Documentation
    Supports the appropriate recording, updating, and maintenance of health information to assist in the monitoring of health conditions, continuity of care, and appropriate referral.
  9. Referral and Continuity of Care
    Facilitates appropriate coordination and referral with hospitals, clinics, medical and dental professionals, and other health facilities when specialized examination, treatment, or follow-up care is required.
  10. Emergency Preparedness and Response
    Promotes preparedness for medical emergencies through appropriate emergency procedures, coordination, first response measures, and referral to appropriate health facilities when necessary.
  11. Health and Wellness Activities
    Encourages appropriate physical wellness, health awareness, hygiene, recreational, and other activities that promote positive health practices and productive use of time.
  12. Health Partnerships and Community Support
    Strengthens coordination with government health agencies, local government units, hospitals, healthcare professionals, civic organizations, private partners, and other stakeholders to support the delivery of appropriate health and wellness services.

Health and Wellness as Part of Rehabilitation

Health and wellness are important components of the rehabilitation process. By promoting access to appropriate health services, preventive care, health education, and wellness activities, the program helps PDLs develop greater awareness of their health and adopt positive and responsible health practices while in custody.

Maintaining good health also helps PDLs participate more effectively in education, skills development, values formation, livelihood activities, and other rehabilitation programs offered within the jail.

The program further promotes a culture of health, hygiene, responsibility, and care for others within the custodial environment. Through appropriate health services and partnerships, it contributes to the humane treatment, rehabilitation, personal development, and successful reintegration of PDLs into their families and communities.

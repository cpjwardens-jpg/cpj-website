Livelihood Development Program

The Livelihood Development Program of the Cagayan Provincial Jail provides qualified Persons Deprived of Liberty (PDLs) with meaningful opportunities to participate in productive, skills- based, and livelihood-oriented activities while in custody. The program aims to develop practical abilities, work values, creativity, responsibility, and productivity that may help PDLs prepare for possible livelihood opportunities after release.

Livelihood activities are designed to complement education, technical-vocational training, values formation, health and wellness, and other rehabilitation programs. Through meaningful work and productive activities, PDLs are encouraged to use their time constructively while developing competencies that may contribute to personal development, family support, and successful reintegration into the community.

Objectives of the Program

The Livelihood Development Program seeks to:

  1. Develop Practical Livelihood Skills
    Provide opportunities for PDLs to acquire and strengthen practical skills that may be useful for livelihood, employment, or self-employment after release.
  2. Promote Productive Use of Time
    Encourage meaningful and constructive activities that promote productivity, discipline, responsibility, and positive use of time while in custody.
  3. Strengthen Work Values
    Develop positive work attitudes such as discipline, perseverance, cooperation, responsibility, quality consciousness, and commitment to completing assigned tasks.
  4. Encourage Creativity and Self-Reliance
    Provide opportunities for PDLs to develop creativity, resourcefulness, problem-solving abilities, and practical approaches to livelihood activities.
  5. Prepare for Future Livelihood Opportunities
    Help PDLs identify practical livelihood possibilities and develop competencies that may support productive activities after release.

Livelihood Activities

The Cagayan Provincial Jail may facilitate and support various livelihood-oriented activities based on available resources, skills, facilities, training opportunities, partnerships, and applicable jail policies. These may include:

  1. Handicraft and Product Making
    Provides opportunities for PDLs to develop skills in making appropriate handicrafts, decorative items, useful products, and other approved materials using available resources.
  2. Organic Vegetable Production
    Provides practical experience in vegetable gardening, preparation of planting areas, seedling management, cultivation, maintenance, harvesting, and basic post-harvest handling of organically grown vegetables.
  3. Food Preparation and Food Products
    Provides opportunities to develop practical skills related to food preparation, processing, packaging, sanitation, proper food handling, and other appropriate food-related livelihood activities.
  4. Skills-Based Livelihood Activities
    Encourages PDLs who have completed technical-vocational or other skills training to apply appropriate competencies through approved productive activities.
  5. Product Development
    Encourages participants to improve the quality, usefulness, presentation, and appropriate packaging of products created through livelihood activities.
  6. Basic Enterprise Awareness
    Introduces appropriate concepts related to livelihood planning, simple costing, resource management, productivity, customer needs, and responsible use of available resources.
  7. Work Values and Productivity
    Integrates discipline, teamwork, responsibility, time management, cooperation, quality of work, and other positive work values into livelihood activities.
  8. Partnership and Community Support
    Encourages coordination with appropriate government agencies, local government units, training institutions, organizations, civic groups, private partners, and other stakeholders that may support livelihood training, materials, mentoring, or other appropriate assistance.

Livelihood Development and Self-Reliance

Livelihood development can help PDLs recognize the value of productive work, responsibility, and self-reliance. By providing opportunities to learn and practice practical skills, the program supports personal development and encourages participants to consider constructive livelihood options for the future.

Where appropriate and subject to applicable rules and arrangements, livelihood activities may also provide opportunities for PDLs to contribute to productive projects and develop experience in completing tasks, working with others, managing resources, and maintaining quality standards.

Livelihood as Part of Rehabilitation

Livelihood development is an important component of rehabilitation because productive activities can help PDLs develop practical competencies while strengthening discipline, responsibility, patience, cooperation, and positive work attitudes.

The program complements education and technical-vocational training by providing opportunities for PDLs to apply knowledge and skills in appropriate productive activities. It also complements values formation by promoting responsibility, perseverance, teamwork, respect for work, and constructive decision-making.

Through meaningful livelihood activities, the Cagayan Provincial Jail seeks to help qualified PDLs develop practical skills, positive work values, and greater readiness for productive opportunities and responsible participation in their families and communities after release.
